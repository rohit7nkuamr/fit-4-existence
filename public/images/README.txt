IMAGE DIRECTORY STRUCTURE

This directory contains all images used in the Fit 4 Existence website. Here's where to place different types of images:

1. /images/doctors/
   - Profile photos of Dr. Prince Raj and other medical staff
   - Recommended size: 600px × 800px (portrait orientation)
   - Format: JPG or PNG

2. /images/services/
   - Images for each service offered (physiotherapy, rehabilitation, etc.)
   - Recommended size: 800px × 600px (landscape orientation)
   - Format: JPG or PNG

3. /images/about/
   - Images for the About Us section (clinic photos, equipment, etc.)
   - Recommended size: 1200px × 800px (landscape orientation)
   - Format: JPG or PNG

4. /images/hero/
   - Large banner images for the homepage hero section
   - Recommended size: 1920px × 1080px (16:9 ratio)
   - Format: JPG or PNG

5. /images/testimonials/
   - Patient testimonial profile photos
   - Recommended size: 200px × 200px (square)
   - Format: JPG or PNG

6. /images/icons/
   - Small icons and logos used throughout the site
   - Recommended size: varies, typically 24px-64px
   - Format: SVG preferred for scalability, PNG with transparency as alternative

IMPORTANT NOTES:
- Use descriptive filenames with lowercase letters and hyphens (e.g., "knee-therapy.jpg")
- Compress all images to reduce file size without losing quality
- For photos with people, ensure you have proper permission to use them
- Keep backup copies of original images before compression
