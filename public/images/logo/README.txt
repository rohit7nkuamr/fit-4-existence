LOGO IMAGE REQUIREMENTS

Place your logo image in this directory with the following specifications:

FILE NAME:
- logo.png

REQUIREMENTS:
- Format: PNG with transparency
- Shape: Preferably circular or square with rounded corners
- Size: At least 200x200 pixels (larger is better for high-resolution displays)
- Background: Transparent background is recommended
- Colors: Use your brand colors (gold and white are currently used in the site)

USAGE:
This logo will be displayed in both the header and footer of the website.
The logo will be automatically resized based on where it appears:
- Small (32x32px) - Used in mobile navigation
- Medium (48x48px) - Used in desktop navigation and footer
- Large (64x64px) - Used in larger components
- Extra Large (96x96px) - Used for special sections

IMPORTANT:
If your logo is not circular, you may need to adjust the CSS in the Logo.jsx component
to remove the "rounded-full" class or modify other styling as needed.
